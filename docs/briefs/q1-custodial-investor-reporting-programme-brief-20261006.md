## PART I -- THE OUTCOME TERRITORY

### The candidate thread

**One 49 Financial advisor's quarter-end client report, produced by Opal from Schwab's records, in place of the Black
Diamond report.** For one advisor, every client's report for the quarter ending 2026-12-31 is generated by Opal from
the custodian's own data, on that firm's template, and the advisor would send it without opening Black Diamond. Every
other outcome below widens this one: more advisors, Cottonwood, more report kinds.

### O1 -- An advisor at 49 Financial sends a client their performance report without opening Black Diamond

Returns, holdings, activity and fees for each account and household, for the standard periods, computed by Opal from
Schwab's custodial records, in a report the advisor can hand a client.

### O2 -- Each advisor firm gets its reports in its own template

49 Financial's and Cottonwood's reports look the way each firm presents itself: sections, periods, benchmarks, wording
and branding chosen per firm, not one Opal layout for everyone.

### O3 -- An advisor at Cottonwood sees a client's history as one continuous record, from 2013 to today

The 13-year Commonwealth history (to 2026-06-23) and the daily Black Diamond data after the move appear as one account
history: one set of returns, one transaction list, with no gap and no double-count at the hand-over. This is the
adapter Andrew named.

### O4 -- Anyone can show, figure by figure, how Opal's numbers compare with Black Diamond's during the hand-over

Until the switch, a person can see where Opal's figure matches Black Diamond's and, where it differs, the named reason.
After the switch, the comparison is the record of why Opal's figures are trusted. (Andrew's word bars a STANDING
comparator; this is the hand-over comparison, which ends at the switch.)

### O5 -- Every security in a client's report is classified and priced from Opal's own securities record

Every holding has its class, sub-class, sector and history from the one classification table and the securities
database, never from Black Diamond's labels.

### O6 -- Andrew can see, at any time, how far each firm is from switching off Black Diamond

One view per firm: which reports are produced by Opal, which still come from Black Diamond, and what is left.

### O7 -- Inflection's reports follow, from Orion, after the two priority firms

Inflection is in scope but last ("not a huge priority"); its custodial path is the Orion feed Opal already holds.

- **The date is Andrew's: 2027-01-01.** Working back from it, the quarter ending 2026-12-31 is the first report Opal
  produces in Black Diamond's place, so the first full dry run has to happen on a real quarter-end before then
  (2026-09-30 is the only one available).

  feed until a direct path exists.

  come from wherever billing ends up.

  that is the CTO's.
- **Capacity:** this programme's projects take most of dell1 and dell2 (four-box plan sections 5-6).
- **No shadow pipeline:** Opal's reports replace Black Diamond's; the hand-over comparison (O4) ends at the switch.
